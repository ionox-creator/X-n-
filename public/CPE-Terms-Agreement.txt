Zero-Data Architecture — Core Principle
CPE is built on a zero-data collection architecture. This is not a feature — it is a fundamental design
philosophy. No data leaves your device. Ever.
3.1 No Data Collection. Ionox Technologies does not collect, store, process, transmit, or

3.3 Local-Only Storage. Any data temporarily stored by the Software (such as chat history
or calculation inputs) is stored exclusively in your browser&apos;s local storage (localStorage/sessionStorage)

permanently deleted when you close your browser tab or window. Data in localStorage persists across sessions
on your device but can be manually cleared at any time through your browser settings or the
application&apos;s built-in data management features.
3.5 No Cookies for Tracking. The Software does not use cookies, web beacons, pixel
trackers, fingerprinting, or any other tracking technology. The only browser storage mechanisms used are
localStorage and sessionStorage for local application state.
3.6 AI Processing. AI-powered features (chat, analysis) process your queries locally or
through encrypted API calls that do not retain, log, or store your input data on any server. AI responses
are generated in real-time and are not archived.
3.7 Compliance. This zero-data architecture inherently complies with GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, and
all applicable data protection regulations because no personal data is collected or processed by us.
