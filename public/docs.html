4.1 Priced Round

Models a priced equity round — the most common type of venture funding.

Inputs:

  • Pre-Money Valuation — Company's valuation before investment (e.g., $10,000,000). This is what investors and founders agree the company is worth before new money enters.
  • Investment Amount — How much the investor is putting in (e.g., $2,000,000). This is the actual cash the investor wires to the company.
  • Option Pool (%) — Employee stock option pool percentage (e.g., 15%). Standard is 10-15%. Pre-money pools dilute founders; post-money pools dilute everyone.

Outputs:

  • Post-Money Valuation = Pre-Money + Investment (e.g., $12M)
  • Investor Equity Share = (Investment ÷ Post-Money) × 100 (e.g., 16.67%)
  • Founder Combined Share = 100% − Investor % − Option Pool % (e.g., 68.33%)

Example: Pre-Money $10M, Investment $2M, Pool 15% → Post-Money $12M, Investor 16.67%, Founders 68.33%

Verdict: The system evaluates the round structure and provides a health assessment:

HEALTHY Round within SEC Reg D corridors, founder share reasonable CAUTION Pool >15%, investor >40%, or founders <50% DANGER Pool >20% carves excessive founder value

References: SEC Regulation D Rule 506; YC SAFE Standard 2018; PG "The Equity Equation"

4.2 SAFE Radar

Analyzes SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity) notes — convertible instruments that defer valuation to the priced round.

Inputs:

  • Valuation Cap — Maximum valuation at which the SAFE converts (e.g., $5,000,000). Protects investors from high future valuations.
  • Investment Amount — How much the investor is putting in via SAFE (e.g., $500,000).
  • SAFE Type — Pre-Money (founder-favorable, shares dilution proportionally) or Post-Money (YC 2018 standard, founders bear full dilution).

Outputs:

  • Ownership at Cap = Investment ÷ Cap × 100 (e.g., $500K ÷ $5M = 10%)
  • Implied Price per Share = Cap ÷ (Investment ÷ Ownership × 100)

Example: Cap $5M, Investment $500K → Ownership 10% at cap

Verdict:

ACCEPTABLE Ownership within early-stage corridors (<15%) MODERATE 15-25% ownership, founders absorb full dilution HIGH RISK >25% ownership, stacked SAFEs compound dilution

References: YC SAFE Guidance Booklet 2018; SEC Regulation D Rule 506

4.3 Waterfall

Models liquidation waterfall — how proceeds are distributed at exit/sale.

Inputs: Exit Value, Investor Preference, Investor %, Founder %, Option Pool %

Outputs: Preference payout, remaining proceeds split by ownership, investor total + multiple, founder total

Example: Exit $50M, Pref $5M, Inv 20%, Founders 70%, Pool 10% → Investor gets $14M (2.80x), Founders get $31.5M

4.4 Vesting

Generates year-by-year vesting schedules for founder/employee equity.

Inputs: Total Shares, Cliff (years), Vesting Period (years)

Standard: 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff (industry norm per Feld & Mendelson)

Example: 100,000 shares, 1yr cliff, 4yr period → 2,083 shares/month. Year 1 (cliff) = 25,000 (25%), Year 2 = 50,000 (50%), etc.

4.5 Anti-Dilution

Models Full Ratchet vs Weighted Average anti-dilution protection for down rounds.

Full Ratchet: Investor's conversion price drops to the new (lower) round price — most investor-friendly.

Weighted Average: Conversion price adjusts based on a weighted formula — more founder-favorable.

5. Console — 20 Commands

The Console is a command-line interface for quick venture finance calculations. Type a command in the input box and press RUN, or click any quick-action button below the input.


Each command returns structured output with sections, data rows, verdicts, and references.

Audit Commands

audit --pre 10M --inv 2M --pool 15 --cur USD safe --cap 5M --inv 500K --cur USD captable --founders 80 --inv 15 --pool 5

audit — Models a priced round. Calculates post-money, investor %, founder %, pool impact. Returns verdict based on SEC Reg D corridors.

safe — Models a SAFE note. Calculates ownership at cap, implied price per share. Supports pre/post-money types.

captable — Generates a cap table summary with share counts. Validates ownership totals = 100%.

Exit & Return Commands

waterfall --exit 50M --pref 5M --inv 20 --founders 70 --pool 10 backsolve --target 50M --exit 80M,120M,200M --rounds 2 --current 80 roi --inv 2M --exit 50M --pct 20 irr --flows -10M,2M,3M,5M,8M

waterfall — Liquidation waterfall. Shows how exit proceeds split between investors, founders, and pool. Calculates investor multiple (e.g., 2.80x).

backsolve — Reverse exit solver. Enter desired founder payout and exit scenarios. Calculates required ownership %, max dilution budget, and per-round dilution.

roi — Investor ROI analysis. Calculates payout, profit, ROI %, and return multiple. Benchmarks: 3x+ good, 10x+ home run.

irr — Internal Rate of Return. Uses NPV=0 solver. Benchmarks: 15%+ minimum, 25%+ strong.

Equity Commands

vesting --shares 100000 --cliff 1 --period 4 dilute --current 80 --rounds 3 --each 20

vesting — Year-by-year vesting schedule. Shows vested shares and % at each year. Standard: 4yr/1yr cliff.

dilute — Forward dilution projection. Formula: remaining = current × (1 − each/100)^rounds. Shows impact of multiple funding rounds.

Cash & Valuation Commands

runway --cash 2M --burn 150K valuation --method vc --rev 2M --growth 50 --exit-mult 10 --years 5 valuation --method multiple --rev 2M --mult 8

runway — Cash runway calculator. Months = Cash ÷ Burn. Standard: 18-24 months post-raise.

valuation — Two methods: VC Method (PostMoney = ExitValue ÷ TargetROI) and Revenue Multiple (Valuation = Revenue × Multiple).

Currency Commands

convert 1000 USD to EUR fx USD PKR currency

convert — Currency conversion with live rates. fx — Exchange rate between two currencies. currency — Lists all 8+ supported currencies.

Reference Commands

explain liquidation preference explain vesting citations

explain — Financial glossary with 20+ venture finance terms. Each term includes a definition and reference.

citations — Shows all reference sources (SEC Reg D, YC SAFE, PG Equity Equation, Feld & Mendelson).

Utility Commands

help — Show all commands status — System diagnostics brain "text" — Inject custom AI instructions clear — Clear console screen

12. Glossary

Key venture finance terms (also available via explain command in Console):

  • Pre-Money Valuation — Company value before investment. Pre-Money + Investment = Post-Money.
  • Post-Money Valuation — Company value after investment.
  • Liquidation Preference — Right of preferred shareholders to receive investment back before common shareholders in exit.
  • Participating Preferred — Investors get preference AND share in remaining (double-dip).
  • Anti-Dilution — Protection for investors if future round is at lower valuation (down round).
  • Vesting — Earning equity over time. Standard: 4-year vesting, 1-year cliff.
  • SAFE — Simple Agreement for Future Equity (YC, 2013). Defers valuation to priced round.
  • Valuation Cap — Maximum valuation for SAFE/convertible note conversion.
  • Option Pool — Shares reserved for employees. Typically 10-15%.
  • Pro-Rata — Right to invest in future rounds to maintain ownership %.
  • Cap Table — Capitalization table showing all stakeholders and ownership.
  • Fully Diluted — Ownership using ALL shares including reserved.
